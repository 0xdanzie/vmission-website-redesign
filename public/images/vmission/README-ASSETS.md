# Vedanta Mission — Real Photo Asset Index

All photographs in this tree are real photographs of Vedanta Ashram, Indore,
its Acharyas, and its activities — supplied for this project. None are
AI-generated. See `scripts/reorganize-assets.js` (project root) for the
provenance mapping from original filenames.

## hero/
- `vmission-hero-cinematic.jpg` — 2048px panoramic cinematic composition of the
  Vedanta Ashram facade, Shivling dome, Poojya Swami Atmananda Saraswati, Swaminis,
  and satsang seekers under warm sunset skies. **Primary cinematic homepage hero artwork.**
- `ashram-facade-dome.jpg` — street-level view of the terracotta Ashram
  building with the white Gangeshwar Mahadev (Shivling) dome and
  "वेदान्त आश्रम" signage. Preserved as alternate/fallback asset.
- `ashram-facade-elevated.jpg` — elevated angle of the same facade, dome,
  and a secondary small dome. Preserved as gallery/fallback asset.

## entrance/
- `ashram-entrance-cinematic.jpg` — high-fidelity panoramic perspective of the
  Ashram terrace courtyard, carved portal arch, open sanctum doors, and illuminated
  Shiva Linga under the consecrated dome at sunset. **Primary unified continuous entrance environment.**

## teaching/
Seven restored historical/legacy teaching and lineage slider photographs:
- `01-swami-atmananda-teaching-restored.jpg` — Poojya Swami Atmananda Saraswati teaching
  on "Tat Tvam Asi" (*You are not an individual, but the infinite self-effulgent existence*).
- `02-swami-atmananda-wisdom-restored.jpg` — Poojya Swami Atmananda Saraswati on "Aham Asmi"
  (*The experience of 'I Am' reveals the substratum - the real me*).
- `03-swamini-teaching-01-restored.jpg` — Swamini Samatananda Saraswati on "Shraddhavan Labhate Jnanam"
  (*Those who have an open & positive mind alone are available for teaching*).
- `04-swamini-teaching-02-restored.jpg` — Swamini Amitananda Saraswati on Kena Upanishad 1.4
  (*Brahman comes neither in the category of 'Known', nor even in the 'Unknown'*).
- `05-acharya-lineage-restored.jpg` — Swamini Poornananda Saraswati & Guruji on "Acharyavan Purusho Veda"
  (*One who can surrender at the feet of a Guru alone can be initiated to appreciate that which transcends the ego*).
- `06-acharya-family-restored.jpg` — Acharya Lineage of Vedanta Ashram on "Vande Guru Paramparam"
  (Swami Atmananda Saraswati, Swamini Amitananda, Swamini Samatananda, Swamini Poornananda).
- `07-vedanta-archive-restored.jpg` — Historical Vedanta Pravachan Archive on "Yogah Karmasu Kaushalam"
  (*The capacity to retain your equipoise in all circumstances is real Yoga*).

## acharyas/
- `guruji-portrait-riverside.jpg` — the strongest, highest-resolution
  Guruji portrait supplied (706×466). **Preferred portrait wherever a large
  Acharya image is needed.**
- `guruji-teaching-closeup.jpg` — direct-address teaching portrait, used in
  the homepage "Teaching" section.

  proposal as a fresh-photography request for a production build.

## events/, publications/

for consistency; content unchanged. Captions burned into the event photos
(e.g. "Talk organized by Rotary Club, Mumbai") were read directly off the
images and used verbatim as factual captions — nothing invented.

## Removed
`hero/vmission-ashram-hero.jpg` was deleted. It was a photograph of Devi
Ahilyabai Holkar Airport, Indore — not the Ashram — and was unused in the
live prototype. Flagged in the discovery audit; removed here so no future
contributor uses it by mistake.
