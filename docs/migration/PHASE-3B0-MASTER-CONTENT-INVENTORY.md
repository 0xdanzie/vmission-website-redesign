## 1. Inventory Method & Schema

This master content inventory documents every discrete page, resource, media item, data record, and external asset discovered from the legacy WordPress website (`vmission.org.in`), previous Phase 1/2 audit registers, and the active Next.js repository.

### Classification Actions:
- `KEEP`: Existing content/asset is correct and remains in place.
- `MERGE`: Duplicate or overlapping content consolidated into a single canonical resource.
- `MIGRATE`: Valid, valuable legacy content/asset brought into the approved architecture.
- `ARCHIVE`: Historical or past material preserved for reference but de-emphasized from primary UI.
- `REWRITE`: Valid meaning requiring structural, editorial, or pedagogical rewriting.
- `REPLACE`: Obsolete or poor asset replaced with verified better asset.
- `REMOVE`: Obsolete, broken, malformed, or irrelevant item permanently decommissioned.
- `VERIFY`: Client or source confirmation strictly required before public display or use.
