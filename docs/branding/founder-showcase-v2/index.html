Art Direction Ruling: CONCEPT A (Cinematic Full-Bleed Editorial)

Concept A solves the core creative challenge by eliminating the "card mentality" completely. By treating the real photograph as the environmental world of the hero, the river's negative space naturally hosts the typography while Guruji stands as the undeniable spiritual focal point. The natural saffron robes supply authentic Bhagwa identity without requiring artificial color grading.
