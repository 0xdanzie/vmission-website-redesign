## 1. Visual Authenticity Standard

Vedanta Ashram in Indore is a living, working Gurukula and spiritual retreat center. The photographic direction must capture the **tangible reality of the Ashram**—its serene architecture, warm sunlight, reverent worship, and intellectual study—rather than idealized or synthetic spiritual imagery.

### Core Visual Principles:
* **Real People & Real Ashram Spaces:** Every primary photograph must depict the actual Vedanta Ashram premises in Indore, its resident Acharyas, or authentic satsang attendees.
* **Documentary & Candid Tone:** Prefer unposed, natural moments of satsang, scriptural reading, chanting, and quiet reflection over stiff, artificial staging.
* **Natural Light & Sacred Warmth:** Leverage natural Indian daylight—morning sun on the white Gangeshwar Mahadev dome, warm afternoon rays in the courtyard, and quiet lamplight during evening aarti.

---

## 2. Strict Imagery Anti-Patterns (What Must Never Be Used)

1. **NO AI-Generated People or Saints:** Synthetic faces, uncanny AI-generated meditation figures, or computerized "enlightenment" illustrations destroy institutional credibility and are strictly banned.
2. **NO Generic Stock Spirituality:** No generic Western models sitting in lotus posture on cliffs, random clip-art Buddha statues, or generic incense sticks on black rocks.
3. **NO Unrelated Temples:** No photographs of Varanasi ghats, Himalayan shrines, or South Indian temples passed off as Vedanta Ashram.
4. **NO Artificial Divine Halos:** No photoshop lens flares, sparkling magical glows, or floating heavenly beams added to portraits or deities.
5. **NO Decorative Sanskrit Wallpaper:** Avoid tiling random Sanskrit letters as meaningless textured background wallpaper.

## 3. Specific Asset Category Guidelines

### 3.1 Acharya Portraits
* **Standard:** Dignified, professional, yet approachable and warm.
* **Primary Reference Asset:** `public/images/vmission/acharyas/guruji-portrait-riverside.jpg` (Poojya Guruji Swami Atmananda Saraswati by the river).
* **Composition:** Medium close-up, soft natural outdoor lighting, authentic saffron robes, warm engaging eye contact.
* **Production Request:** The current Swamini portraits (`swamini-*.jpg`) are legacy small cutouts (300×280px). A production photoshoot should capture matching natural, high-resolution portraits of all three Swaminijis.

### 3.2 Ashram Architecture & Sacred Spaces
* **The Consecrated Dome:** The monumental white Gangeshwar Mahadev (Shivling) dome on the terracotta roof is the unique architectural fingerprint of the Ashram (`ashram-facade-dome.jpg`, `gangeshwar-dome-closeup.jpg`). It must be depicted clearly as a monumental Shivling, not a generic cupola.
* **The Sacred Threshold:** The hand-carved wooden sanctum doors with Nandi statue (`sanctum-doors-threshold.jpg`) serve as the visual threshold of welcome.
* **The Teaching Hall:** Wide interior shots of the discourse hall (`teaching-hall-interior.jpg`) showing carpets, low desks, and study atmosphere.

### 3.3 Teachings Media Thumbnails
* **Aspect Ratio:** Fixed 16:9 widescreen ratio.
* **Treatment:** Subtle dark gradient overlay on the lower third for title legibility. Clean scriptural theme icon or verified Acharya portrait.
* **Consistency:** Unified font sizing and badge placement across all audio and video thumbnails.

### 3.4 Publications & Periodicals
* **Cover Display:** High-resolution vertical 3:4 aspect ratio.
* **Realism:** Rendered with a gentle paper texture and soft drop shadow (`--vm-shadow-md`) to evoke the tactile feel of an authentic printed book or e-zine.
* **Preservation:** Preserve the original cover art of historical *Vedanta Sandesh* and *Vedanta Articles* volumes without distortion.

### 3.5 Events & Activities
* **Authentic Captions:** Real event photographs (such as discourses in Mumbai, Indore Shivirs, and rural medical camps) must retain their factual, verbatim captions read directly from original prints (`public/images/vmission/README-ASSETS.md`).
