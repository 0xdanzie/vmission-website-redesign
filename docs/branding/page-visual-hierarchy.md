## 1. Homepage (`/`)
* **Primary Objective:** Deliver an immersive, contemplative welcome; introduce the sacred mission; present direct gateways to the Teachings library, latest publications, and the Indore Ashram.
* **Hero Section:** Virtual Ashram Entrance / panoramic sunset view of the Gangeshwar Mahadev dome, sacred threshold doors, and Poojya Guruji. Headline: *"Spreading Love & Light by revealing the basic oneness of all"*.
* **Major Sections:**
  1. Daily Darshan / Contemplative Shloka Banner
  2. The Three Pillars: Satsang (Wisdom), Swadhyaya (Study), Seva (Selfless Service)
  3. Featured Teachings Slider (Gita & Upanishad Pravachans)
  4. Latest Publications Spotlight (*Vedanta Sandesh* current issue + E-Book)
  5. Ashram Invitation & Indore Pilgrimage Teaser
* **CTA Hierarchy:** Primary: *"Explore Teachings"* (Terracotta button); Secondary: *"Plan a Visit"* (Brass outline); Tertiary: *"Donate / Seva"*.
* **Visual Density:** Spacious, cinematic, editorial.

## 2. About Hub (`/about`)
* **Primary Objective:** Establish the philosophical tradition, founding history, legal governance, and lineage credibility of Vedanta Mission.
* **Hero Section:** Elegant text header on ivory sand with historical ashram photograph. Title: *"Advaita Vedanta Tradition & Governance"*.
* **Major Sections:** Vision & Mission narrative (`#vision`), Adi Shankaracharya Lineage overview, Acharyas directory intro, Legal Trusts (`#trusts` — VPST & ICF), Satsang Centers overview (`#centers`).
* **Visual Density:** Medium density; scholarly reading atmosphere.

---

## 3. Acharyas Hub & Profiles (`/acharyas` & `/acharyas/[id]`)
* **Primary Objective:** Introduce the monastic teachers carrying forward the Advaita tradition under Poojya Guruji.
* **Hub Layout (`/acharyas`):** 4-card gallery featuring Poojya Guruji Swami Atmanandaji and the three Swaminijis.
* **Detail Profile (`/acharyas/[id]`):** Asymmetrical 2-column layout: Left column features large portrait and lineage titles; Right column features verified biographical text, teaching focus, and a direct curated link: *"Listen to Guruji's Discourses"*.

## 5. Teachings Library & Player (`/teachings` & `/teachings/[id]`)
* **Primary Objective:** Instant discovery, streaming, and study of audio and video discourses across the 7 canonical categories.
* **Hero Section:** Compact title banner with prominent search bar and quick category pills.
* **Filter Bar:** Multi-faceted selectors: Type (Audio / Video), Category (7 canonical categories), Speaker, Language.
* **Media Grid:** 3-column responsive card grid displaying series titles, durations, and play buttons.
* **Player Experience (`AudioPlayerDock`):** Persistent bottom docked player allowing continuous listening while exploring.

---

## 6. Publications Repository & Reader (`/publications` & `/publications/[id]`)
* **Primary Objective:** Provide instant reading and downloading of *Vedanta Sandesh*, *Vedanta Piyush*, E-Books, and Sanskrit study texts.
* **Tabbed Interface:** Four primary tabs: `Vedanta Sandesh` | `Vedanta Piyush` | `E-Books` | `Study Texts`.
* **Featured Issue Banner:** Highlighting the latest confirmed issue (e.g. March 2026).
* **Archive Shelf:** Grid of book/periodical cards with dual action: "Read Online" (Flipbook modal) and "Download PDF" (direct Archive.org mirror).
* **Newsletter Box:** Integrated subscription module.

---

## 7. Events Calendar & Archive (`/events` & `/events/[id]`)
* **Primary Objective:** Keep devotees informed regarding upcoming Gita Shivirs, residential camps, and festival celebrations.
* **Major Sections:** Forthcoming Programs (Chronological card feed with date badges), Recurring Weekly Ashram Schedule, Earlier Programs & Past Shivir Photo Archive (`#archive`).

---

## 8. Learn Section (`/learn` & `/learn/[id]`)
* **Primary Objective:** Guide seekers through structured, self-paced study paths of Vedanta philosophy.
* **Structure:** Scoped learning tracks linking to curated sequences of discourses in `/teachings` (e.g. "Foundation of Vedanta", "Gita for Daily Life") + residential Gurukula inquiry.

---

## 9. Donate / Seva Hub (`/donate`)
* **Primary Objective:** Foster voluntary patronage and devotional seva in a dignified, secure environment.
* **Tone:** Contemplative, devotional, transparent. Zero aggressive fundraising tactics.
* **Sections:** Spiritual purpose of Seva (Annakshetra, Sadhu Seva, Ashram Upkeep), Payment Methods Grid (Bank Wire, Verified UPI, Overseas Remittance), Tax Exemption Disclosures (80-G status notice), Receipt Request Form.

---

## 10. Contact Desk (`/contact`)
* **Primary Objective:** Provide direct, immediate communication channels for pilgrims, students, and donors.
* **Key Visuals:** Clear address card with verified geo-coordinates, prominent WhatsApp helpline card, official telephone numbers, and an accessible inquiry form.
