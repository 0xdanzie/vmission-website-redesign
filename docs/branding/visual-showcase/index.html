Founder & Principal Acharya · Vedanta Mission

Poojya Swami
Atmananda Saraswati

Poojya Swami Atmanandaji is the founder and principal Acharya of Vedanta Mission and Vedanta Ashram, Indore. A lifelong scholar of Advaita Vedanta in the tradition of Adi Shankaracharya, Guruji has dedicated decades to the study, practice, and teaching of Upanishadic wisdom — conducting Jnana Yagnas, Shibirs, and Pravachans across India and internationally.

His teachings span the Bhagavad Gita (all 18 chapters), the major Upanishads, Prakarana Granth texts including Vivekachudamani and Atmabodha, Meditation, and devotional literature.

Sacred Sanctum

Vedanta Ashram, Indore

Home to the sacred Gangeshwar Mahadev Mandir, the satsang hall, and the living Vedanta Gurukula tradition.

Sacred Architecture

Gangeshwar Mahadev Mandir

The consecrated Shivling sanctum with its distinctive white dome is the spiritual heart of the Ashram — a focal point for daily pujas, Rudra Abhishek, and contemplative prayer.

The courtyard with carved wooden threshold doors opens into the sacred sanctum, where seekers sit in silence or participate in the traditional pujas and chanting rituals that mark the rhythm of Ashram life.

Satsang & Study

The Teaching Hall

The satsang hall hosts daily discourses, chanting sessions, and Vedanta study classes. Seekers gather in quiet reverence to engage with the ancient Upanishadic texts under the guidance of the resident Acharyas.

Bhagwa Presence — Design Range

This comparison helps reviewers understand the calibrated Bhagwa range. Version A is the recommended final balance. Version B shows a stronger Bhagwa emphasis for reference.

Version A — Recommended

Surface distribution: ~60% Ivory · ~20% Walnut · ~15% Bhagwa · ~3% Green · ~2% Brass

Result: Bhagwa identity is unmistakable and commanding — especially in the header, CTAs, active states, and eyebrow badges — while the dominant ivory canvas maintains reading calm and prevents orange fatigue. This is the final approved direction.

Not recommended as the everyday platform default.
