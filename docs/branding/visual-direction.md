## 1. Design Philosophy: "Modern Indian Gurukula — Bhagwa Anchor"

The visual direction for the Vedanta Mission digital platform is grounded in an authentic spiritual and cultural ratio:
> **60% Calm Temple Ivory & Sandalwood + 20% Scholarly Dark Walnut + 15% Sacred Bhagwa Anchor + 3% Ashram Nature Green + 2% Antique Brass**

Advaita Vedanta is an ancient, rigorous, contemplative intellectual and spiritual tradition. The digital platform evokes the consecrated atmosphere of the physical Ashram in Indore — its sunlit courtyards, sacred Gangeshwar Mahadev sanctum, Vedic chants, and quiet library — while providing the reading comfort, typography, and speed of a world-class literary publication.

In this refined and frozen baseline, **Bhagwa (Saffron / Gerua)** is unmistakably the principal spiritual brand color:
* **Bhagwa (Primary `#E06328` / CTA `#C84E17`):** Spiritual identity & living tradition
* **Temple Ivory (`#FDFBF7`):** Wisdom, sacred parchment & reading calm (*Swadhyaya*)
* **Deep Bilva Green (`#2D4F38`):** Ashram nature, peace & grounding
* **Midnight Walnut (`#1E1916`):** Philosophical scholarship, monastic weight & Shastra authority
* **Antique Brass (`#C5A059`):** Consecrated temple diya & sacred detail

The surface balance percentages are a **design-balance guideline**, not a literal per-page mathematical requirement.

## 2. Six Core Brand Personality Traits

Every visual decision (color, type, spacing, motion) is directly derived from these six core traits:

### 1. Contemplative & Serene
* **Visual Expression:** Generous whitespace, unhurried vertical rhythm, warm ivory backgrounds (`#FDFBF7`) that reduce eye fatigue during study, and delicate stone borders.
* **Avoidance:** Chaotic popups, high-saturation flashing banners, or noisy commercial layouts.

### 2. Authentic & Rooted in Tradition
* **Visual Expression:** Sacred Bhagwa (`#E06328`) and Antique Temple Brass (`#C5A059`) inspired by consecrated ashram stones, brass lamps, and sannyasi robes. Authentic Devanagari typography (`ॐ`) alongside classical Sanskrit citations.
* **Avoidance:** Generic Western clip art, artificial neon gradients, or generic "lotus flower" stock templates.
* *[Historical note: Phase 2D originally referenced `#D95D39` Terracotta Saffron. That value is retired. See `PHASE-2D6-BHAGWA-BRAND-REFINEMENT.md` §3 for the refinement rationale.]*

### 3. Scholarly & Manuscript-Adjacent
* **Visual Expression:** Deep literary serif typography (`Cormorant Garamond`), elegant drop caps, dignified section dividers, verse numbering, and footnotes.
* **Avoidance:** Oversized bubbly cartoon sans-serifs or hyper-modern tech-startup fonts.
* *[Historical note: Phase 2D originally listed `Fraunces` as display and `Inter` as UI. Both are retired. See `PHASE-2D6-BHAGWA-BRAND-REFINEMENT.md` §6 for retirement rationale.]*

### 4. Warm & Welcoming
* **Visual Expression:** Natural, candid photography of satsang gatherings, smiling resident sevaks, sunlit trees, and warm candlelight. Micro-interactions with soft amber glows (`--vm-brass-glow`).
* **Avoidance:** Cold sterile corporate minimalism, distant austere monoliths, or unwelcoming religious dogma.

### 5. Institutionally Credible & Trustworthy
* **Visual Expression:** Symmetrical desktop layouts, clear legal trust governance, structured publication archives, transparent seva options, and secure bank card styling.
* **Avoidance:** Gimmicky gamification, fake countdown timers, or pressure-driven donation banners.

### 6. Dignified & Restrained
* **Visual Expression:** Thoughtful, purposeful visual hierarchy. Elements are given room to breathe. Sacred texts are framed with quiet reverence. Bhagwa is calibrated to approximately 15% of surface area — commanding but not overwhelming.
* **Avoidance:** Gaudy gold leafing, excessive animated deity halos, decorative visual noise, generic orange UI, or political/tricolor associations.

## 3. What the Design Is vs. What It Must Never Be

| The Design IS: | The Design MUST NEVER BE: |
|---|---|
| A calm, timeless digital Gurukula | A high-pressure SaaS dashboard or startup landing page |
| A scholarly repository of sacred wisdom | Generic orange UI or commercial crowdfunding design |
| An authentic, documentary photographic portal | An AI-generated synthetic "spiritual" fantasy |
| A Sanatan-rooted, Vedantic institution | Political branding or tricolor-themed design |
| An accessible, readable digital library | An overly ornate, unreadable religious poster |
| A Bhagwa-anchored, contemplative experience | A generic temple-template or devotional-poster design |
